Paint to erase leftovers or bring back missing parts. Mask view shows removed areas in red.

Your photo stays on this device. The model runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Models: BiRefNet lite (MIT licence) for the cutout, EfficientSAM (Apache 2.0) for picking objects and MI-GAN (MIT licence) for filling removed areas, run with ONNX Runtime Web. Example photo by Rachel Michetti (CC0).

Shortcuts: V move, W pick objects, X remove objects, Enter remove selection, C crop, E erase, B restore, R result, \ compare, M mask, Space pan, [ and ] brush size, Ctrl+Z undo.
